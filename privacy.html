PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here is how Techie-Tornadoes handles your personal information.

Last updated: August 2026

1. What Data We Collect

When you register for events through our platform, we collect the following information — matching exactly the fields on our registration form:

  • Full Name — to identify you on event passes and certificates.
  • Email Address — for confirmations, login, and event updates.
  • Phone Number — for urgent event communications.
  • College / Organization — to verify student or institutional affiliation.
  • Year of Study & Branch — for demographic reporting and event segmentation.
  • Event Selection — to process your registration for a specific event.
  • Password — stored locally for account login (see Data Storage below).

2. How Data Is Used

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Process and confirm event registrations.
  • Generate registration IDs and digital passes.
  • Enable login to your Techie-Tornadoes account.
  • Send event reminders, schedule updates, and result notifications.
  • Improve our platform and event offerings through anonymised analytics.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Techie-Tornadoes is a frontend-only demo portal with no backend server. All registration data, login credentials, and theme preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage. This means your data remains on your device and is not transmitted to any external server. While convenient for demo purposes, localStorage is not encrypted — please do not use real passwords you use elsewhere. Clearing your browser data will permanently delete your stored registrations.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We offer optional social login buttons for Google, LinkedIn, and GitHub. These integrations are placeholders in the current demo and are not yet connected to live OAuth providers. When fully integrated, authentication would be handled by the respective third-party provider under their own privacy policies. We do not share your registration data with these providers unless you explicitly authorise social sign-in.

5. Cookie Usage

Techie-Tornadoes does not use tracking cookies. We use browser localStorage to persist your dark mode preference (techie_theme) and registration data (techieTornadoesRegistrations). These are functional storage mechanisms, not advertising cookies.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your stored registration data via the "Check Status" tool on the registration page.
  • Request deletion of your data by clearing your browser's localStorage for this site, or by contacting us directly.
  • Opt out of optional communications by not providing a phone number or by contacting us.

7. Contact Information

For privacy-related inquiries or data deletion requests, contact us at privacy@techietornadoes.in or through our Contact page. We respond to all privacy requests within 7 business days.
