About Techie-Tornadoes

Empowering the next generation of innovators through world-class technical events.

Our Mission

Techie-Tornadoes was born from a simple belief: every student deserves access to high-quality technical events that challenge their skills, expand their network, and accelerate their career. What started as a small campus hackathon has grown into one of India's most vibrant student tech communities.

We curate coding competitions, robotics battles, cybersecurity challenges, innovation showcases, and expert-led workshops — all designed to bridge the gap between classroom learning and real-world problem solving. Whether you're a first-year student writing your first line of code or a seasoned builder ready to compete, Techie-Tornadoes is your launchpad.

Built for CodeForge WebSprint 2026, our platform demonstrates how thoughtful frontend design can deliver a complete event management experience — from discovery and registration to community engagement — without a single line of backend code.
